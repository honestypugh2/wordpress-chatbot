# Emergency Services & Alerts (County of Westvale — FICTIONAL)

> Synthetic prototype content. Programs and links are invented.
> **For any real emergency, call 911.**

The Westvale County Office of Emergency Services (OES) coordinates preparedness,
alerts, and response information.

## Westvale Alerts (fictional)
- Opt in to receive evacuation, wildfire, flood, and severe-weather notices by
  **SMS, email, or voice call**.
- Register multiple addresses (home, work, a relative's home).

## Be prepared
- Build a go-bag: water, medications, documents, chargers, and supplies for 3 days.
- Know your **evacuation zone** using the Westvale Zone Lookup tool.
- Sign up for alerts before fire season.

## During an emergency
- Follow official instructions from OES and public-safety agencies.
- Check the Westvale Emergency Dashboard for evacuation orders and shelter locations.
