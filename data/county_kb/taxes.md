# Property Taxes (County of Westvale — FICTIONAL)

> Synthetic prototype content. Dates, amounts, and policies are invented.

The Treasurer-Tax Collector bills and collects secured property taxes for the
County of Westvale.

## Payment schedule (fictional)
Property tax is paid in two installments:
- **First installment:** due **November 1**, delinquent after **December 10**.
- **Second installment:** due **February 1**, delinquent after **April 10**.

A 10% penalty (fictional) applies to late installments.

## Ways to pay
- **Online** with eCheck (no fee) or card (a fictional 2.25% processing fee applies).
- **By mail** with the payment stub from your annual bill.
- **In person** at the Treasurer's office during business hours.

## Common questions
- **Lost your bill?** Reprint it from the Westvale Tax Portal using your parcel number.
- **Escrow/impound?** If your lender pays taxes, confirm they received the bill.
- **Disagree with your assessment?** Contact the Assessor's office to discuss an appeal.
