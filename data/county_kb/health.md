# Public Health & Vital Records (County of Westvale — FICTIONAL)

> Synthetic prototype content. Services, fees, and hours are invented.

Westvale County Public Health operates clinics, issues vital records, and inspects
food facilities.

## Vital records (fictional fees)
- **Birth certificate (certified copy):** $29, request online or in person.
- **Death certificate (certified copy):** $24.
- Valid government ID is required; only authorized persons may request certain records.

## Clinic services (fictional)
- Immunizations and travel vaccines by appointment.
- Communicable disease information and referrals.
- WIC and family-health program referrals.

## Food-facility inspections
- Restaurant inspection results are posted to the Westvale Food Safety portal.
- Report a food-safety concern through the online complaint form.

## Important
For medical emergencies call **911**. This page is general information, not medical advice.
