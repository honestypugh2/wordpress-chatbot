## Report a concern
Use the Westvale 311 portal to report non-emergency issues:
- **Potholes and road maintenance** (Public Works).
- **Illegal dumping** and code violations.
- **Graffiti** on public property.
- **Streetlight outages.**

When you submit a report you receive a tracking number to follow its status.
