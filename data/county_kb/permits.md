# Building & Land-Use Permits (County of Westvale — FICTIONAL)

> All content on this page is synthetic and for prototype demonstration only.

The County of Westvale Department of Public Works issues permits for construction,
remodeling, grading, and encroachment within unincorporated areas of the county.

## Common permit types
- **Residential Building Permit** — new homes, additions, and major remodels.
- **Electrical / Plumbing / Mechanical Permit** — trade work, often a sub-permit.
- **Grading Permit** — earth movement above a fictional 50-cubic-yard threshold.
- **Encroachment Permit** — work within a county right-of-way (sidewalks, driveways).

## How to apply
1. Create an account on the Westvale Online Permit Center (fictional).
2. Submit plans and the application form for your permit type.
3. Pay the application fee (base residential permit fee: **$175**, fictional).
4. Track plan-check status online; respond to any correction notices.

## Typical timelines (fictional)
- Simple residential permits: reviewed within **10 business days**.
- Complex or multi-discipline projects: **4–8 weeks**, depending on revisions.
