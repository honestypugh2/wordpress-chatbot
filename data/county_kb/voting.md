# Voting & Elections (County of Westvale — FICTIONAL)

> Synthetic prototype content. All deadlines and rules are invented.

The Westvale County Elections Office manages voter registration, ballots, and
polling places.

## Register to vote
- Register or update your registration **online** through the state voter portal.
- Fictional deadline: register at least **15 days** before an election.
- **Same-day conditional registration** may be available at designated sites.

## Ways to vote (fictional)
- **Vote by mail:** every active registered voter is mailed a ballot.
- **Drop boxes:** official drop boxes open ~29 days before Election Day.
- **In-person vote centers:** open the weekend before and on Election Day.

## Check your status
- Confirm your registration and ballot status on the Westvale Voter Lookup tool.
- Track your mailed ballot from acceptance to count.
